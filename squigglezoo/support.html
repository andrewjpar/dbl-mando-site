Squiggle Zoo — Support

Squiggle Zoo is a piece of paper that plays back: draw anything and it opens its eyes, grows legs and walks off. It is made for iPad, for children aged about three to six.

How it works

  • It opens straight onto the paper. Draw anything with a finger or Apple Pencil; about a second after you lift it, the drawing comes alive. Its shape decides its personality.
  • Poke a drawing to make it squeak; keep poking for a giggle and a sneeze. Press and hold to pick it up and throw it.
  • The crayon button (top left) opens the crayons. The rainbow crayon gives each new drawing the next colour.
  • The column down the right edge: the trash can, then the sky, animals, dress up, toys, silly things and papers. Tap one to open its tray, then tap a picture or drag it onto the paper. Drop a hat on one drawing to dress just that one.
  • The trash can clears the paper: every drawing leaves its own way and the paper is fresh again.
  • The drawing on the paper is still there next time the app opens. There is nothing to collect and nothing is kept once it leaves.
  • Sound follows the iPad's Silent setting and plays alongside your music.
