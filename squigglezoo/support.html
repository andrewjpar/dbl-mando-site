Squiggle Zoo — Support

Squiggle Zoo is a piece of paper that plays back: draw anything and it opens its eyes, grows legs and walks off. It is made for iPad, for children aged about three to six.

How it works

  • Tap the pencil on the front door to start.
  • Draw with a finger. About a second after you lift it, the drawing comes alive.
  • The palette button (top left) opens the one menu: weather, dress-up, animals, toys, silly things, papers, and home to go back to the front door.
  • The bin (top right) clears the paper.
  • Every drawing is saved on the iPad and is there next time.
