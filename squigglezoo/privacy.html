Squiggle Zoo — Privacy Policy

Squiggle Zoo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

The app has no internet access. It does not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party services. It does not ask for your name, email, location, photos, microphone, or camera. It does not have accounts.

The only data the app keeps is the drawings made on the screen, which are saved on the device itself so they are there the next time the app opens. That data never leaves the device and is removed when the app is deleted.

Squiggle Zoo is designed for children under 13 and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by collecting no information at all.
