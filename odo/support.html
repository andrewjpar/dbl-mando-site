Common questions

A drive wasn't logged

Check three things in Odo's Settings: Location is "Always", Precise Location is on, and Odo hasn't been swiped away in the app switcher — iOS won't wake an app you have closed that way. Odo also looks for drives it might have missed: if one drive ends somewhere the next doesn't start, you'll see "A drive may be missing" on the Drives tab, and Odo can fill it in from a road route.

For a trigger that never misses, open the Shortcuts app, create an automation for "When my iPhone connects to CarPlay" (or your car's Bluetooth), and add Odo's Start a Drive action. It needs Odo's location access set to "Always", because the automation runs while Odo is closed.

One drive was split into two

A stop of more than about four minutes ends a drive — a stop for coffee, say. Open the first drive and choose Join with the next drive.

Will it drain my battery?

While you're parked, Odo uses no GPS at all. GPS runs only while you drive, which is when your phone is most likely to be charging anyway. Settings › Battery shows exactly what Odo uses.

What does "Estimated" mean on a drive?

Part of the distance wasn't measured fix by fix — a tunnel, a stretch with no GPS, or a drive added after the fact. Odo counts the straight line across a gap, which slightly under-counts any road that bends: on a tax log, that's the safe way to be wrong. You can type a corrected distance on any drive; the measured one is kept too.

Why is a drive from home to my office marked as a commute?

Because the IRS says it is one. Driving between your home and a regular place of work isn't deductible business mileage, even on a work day. Odo spots these when you've marked places as Home and Regular workplace, and never classifies them as business on its own.

Which IRS rate does Odo use?

The standard mileage rate for the date of each drive: 70¢ for 2025; 72.5¢ for January to June 2026 and 76¢ from July 1st, 2026, when the IRS raised it mid-year. If your employer reimburses at its own rate, set it in Settings › Mileage rates.

What does Odo Pro add?

Free, Odo logs up to 15 drives a month automatically, plus any drive you start yourself, with your full history and spreadsheet export. Odo Pro logs every drive automatically, adds work-hours rules that classify drives for you, and makes the PDF log.

How do I cancel?

In iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Odo Pro, or from Odo's Settings › Manage subscription. Your drives stay — nothing you've logged is ever hidden.

I have a new iPhone

Restore it from an iCloud Backup of the old one and your log comes with it. Use Settings › Restore purchases if Odo Pro doesn't show as active.

Is this tax advice?

No. Odo keeps an accurate record. What you can deduct depends on your situation — ask an accountant, and give them the PDF.
