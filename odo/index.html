The mileage log that keeps itself

At 72.5¢ to 76¢ a mile in 2026, every business mile you don't log is money you give away. Odo notices when you drive, measures the drive, and asks one question when you park.

Built for people who drive for work

  • Automatic. No start button, no stop button. GPS only while you drive.
  • Notices what it missed. If one drive ends where the next doesn't begin, Odo says so — and fills it in.
  • Classify from your lock screen. Business, with a purpose, or personal, without opening the app.
  • IRS-ready. The right rate for every drive's date, commutes kept out, parking and tolls on top, Schedule C's mileage questions answered.
  • No account. Your log stays on your iPhone. Export it any time, free.
