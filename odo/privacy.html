Odo

Privacy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Odo is a mileage log that knows where you drive. That is sensitive, so it is built to keep it to itself: there is no Odo account, no Odo server, and nothing about your driving is sent to me.

What Odo stores, and where

Your drives — when, where they started and ended, the route, how far, and what you said they were for — along with the places, vehicles and rules you set up. All of it is stored on your iPhone. It is included in your iPhone's iCloud Backup if you use iCloud Backup, under your Apple Account and Apple's protection, and nowhere else.

What leaves your iPhone

  • The start and end of each drive, to Apple Maps, to look up their addresses and nearby business names, and to work out a road route when you fill in a missed drive. Apple's map service receives a coordinate with no account or device identifier from Odo attached. See Apple's Maps privacy notice.
  • Purchases, to Apple. Subscriptions are handled entirely by the App Store. Odo never sees your payment details.
  • Files you choose to share. When you export a log, it goes where you send it and nowhere else.

That is everything. Odo contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no third-party code that sends data anywhere.

What I collect

Nothing. I can't see your drives, your locations, or even how many people use Odo beyond the download and sales figures Apple reports to every developer.

Location

Odo asks for "Always" location so it can notice a drive without being opened. While your car is parked it uses no GPS at all — only the low-power signals your iPhone already tracks to tell when you have left the spot. GPS runs from the moment you drive off until you park. You can switch automatic logging off in Settings, or change Odo's location access in iOS Settings at any time; you can still log drives by hand.

Motion & Fitness

Used on your iPhone to tell when you are in a car rather than walking or cycling. It is never stored by Odo or sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Settings › Your data › Delete all data removes every drive, place and vehicle from your iPhone. Deleting the app does the same. Export your drives first if you may need them: the IRS can ask for records for three years or more.

Children

Odo is a tool for keeping business mileage records and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If Odo ever starts sending data anywhere it does not today, the app will ask you first.
